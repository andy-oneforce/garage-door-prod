Garage Door Repair

Doors come off their tracks, panels bend, hinges wear, and rollers seize. We diagnose the real cause instead of just treating the symptom — most repairs are done in a single visit.

  • Safety inspection of the whole door
  • Spring & cable check
  • Safety-sensor test
  • Early warning on worn springs & cables
  • Off-track and misaligned doors
  • Bent or damaged panels & sections
  • Noisy, jerky, or slow operation
  • Doors that won't fully open or close
Broken Spring Replacement

A snapped torsion or extension spring is the #1 reason a door suddenly won't lift — and it's genuinely dangerous to tackle yourself. We carry the right springs on the truck and replace them safely, in pairs, so the second one doesn't fail a week later.

  • Torsion & extension springs
  • Loud “bang” then a door that won't budge
  • Replaced in matched pairs for even wear
  • Correct spring size & cycle rating for your door
Rollers, Cables & Tracks

The small parts do the quiet work. Worn nylon rollers get noisy, frayed lift cables are a safety risk, and dented tracks make a door bind. Replacing them early is cheap insurance against a door that fails completely.

  • Quiet nylon roller upgrades
  • Frayed or snapped cable replacement
  • Track straightening & realignment
  • Hinge & bracket replacement
Opener Repair & Service

From worn gears to a full replacement, we service all major opener brands. Upgrading? Newer belt-drive units are dramatically quieter and add smartphone control and battery backup for power outages.

  • Opener won't respond / runs then reverses
  • Quiet belt-drive upgrades with Wi-Fi & battery backup
  • Safety-sensor alignment
Garage Door Replacement

Sometimes a door is too far gone to fix sensibly. If so, Danny will tell you straight, and can replace it. Mostly, though, we keep your existing door working.
